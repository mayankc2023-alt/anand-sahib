Pauri 1

Ang 917
About this pauriGuru Amar Das Ji describes the joy of meeting the True Guru. It comes naturally, the mind fills with celebration, and even the musical measures come to sing God's praise.
ਅਨੰਦੁ ਭਇਆ ਮੇਰੀ ਮਾਏ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਮੈ ਪਾਇਆ ॥
Anand bhaya meri maaye, Satguru main paaya
अनंदु भइआ मेरी माए सतिगुरू मै पाइआ ॥
O my mother, I am in bliss, because I have found the True Guru.
ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਤ ਪਾਇਆ ਸਹਜ ਸੇਤੀ ਮਨਿ ਵਜੀਆ ਵਾਧਾਈਆ ॥
Satguru ta paaya sahaj seti, man vajiyaan vadhaaiyaan
सतिगुरु त पाइआ सहज सेती मनि वजीआ वाधाईआ ॥
I found the True Guru with natural ease, and songs of celebration ring in my mind.
ਰਾਗ ਰਤਨ ਪਰਵਾਰ ਪਰੀਆ ਸਬਦ ਗਾਵਣ ਆਈਆ ॥
Raag ratan parvaar pariyaan, sabad gaavan aaiyaan
राग रतन परवार परीआ सबद गावण आईआ ॥
The jewel-like ragas and their families have come to sing the Guru's Word.
ਸਬਦੋ ਤ ਗਾਵਹੁ ਹਰੀ ਕੇਰਾ ਮਨਿ ਜਿਨੀ ਵਸਾਇਆ ॥
Sabado ta gaavo Hari kera, man jini vasaaya
सबदो त गावहु हरी केरा मनि जिनी वसाइआ ॥
Those who have placed the Lord in their mind, sing the Word of the Lord.
ਕਹੈ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਅਨੰਦੁ ਹੋਆ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਮੈ ਪਾਇਆ ॥੧॥
Kahai Nanak anand hoya, Satguru main paaya ‖1‖
कहै नानकु अनंदु होआ सतिगुरू मै पाइआ ॥१॥
Says Nanak, I am in bliss, because I have found the True Guru.
2

Pauri 2

Ang 917
About this pauriThe Guru speaks to his own mind: stay with God always. God takes away pain, accepts you as His own, and sets every task right, so why forget Him?
ਏ ਮਨ ਮੇਰਿਆ ਤੂ ਸਦਾ ਰਹੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਲੇ ॥
Ae man meriya, tu sada rahu Har naale
ए मन मेरिआ तू सदा रहु हरि नाले ॥
O my mind, always stay with the Lord.
ਹਰਿ ਨਾਲਿ ਰਹੁ ਤੂ ਮੰਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਦੂਖ ਸਭਿ ਵਿਸਾਰਣਾ ॥
Har naal rahu tu mann mere, dookh sabh visaarna
हरि नालि रहु तू मंन मेरे दूख सभि विसारणा ॥
Stay with the Lord, O my mind, and He will make all your sorrows disappear.
ਅੰਗੀਕਾਰੁ ਓਹੁ ਕਰੇ ਤੇਰਾ ਕਾਰਜ ਸਭਿ ਸਵਾਰਣਾ ॥
Angeekaar oh kare tera, kaaraj sabh savaarna
अंगीकारु ओहु करे तेरा कारज सभि सवारणा ॥
He will accept you as His own and set all your affairs right.
ਸਭਨਾ ਗਲਾ ਸਮਰਥੁ ਸੁਆਮੀ ਸੋ ਕਿਉ ਮਨਹੁ ਵਿਸਾਰੇ ॥
Sabhna gala samrath suaami, so kiyon manahu visaare
सभना गला समरथु सुआमी सो किउ मनहु विसारे ॥
The Master is able to do all things. Why would you forget Him from your mind?
ਕਹੈ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਮੰਨ ਮੇਰੇ ਸਦਾ ਰਹੁ ਹਰਿ ਨਾਲੇ ॥੨॥
Kahai Nanak mann mere, sada rahu Har naale ‖2‖
कहै नानकु मंन मेरे सदा रहु हरि नाले ॥२॥
Says Nanak, O my mind, always stay with the Lord.

Pauri 3

Ang 917
About this pauriGod's treasury holds everything, and it is given by His grace. Those who keep the Name in their heart live in constant praise, and the divine Word resounds within them.
ਸਾਚੇ ਸਾਹਿਬਾ ਕਿਆ ਨਾਹੀ ਘਰਿ ਤੇਰੈ ॥
Saache saahiba, kya naahi ghar terai
साचे साहिबा किआ नाही घरि तेरै ॥
O True Master, what is there that is not in Your home?
ਘਰਿ ਤ ਤੇਰੈ ਸਭੁ ਕਿਛੁ ਹੈ ਜਿਸੁ ਦੇਹਿ ਸੁ ਪਾਵਏ ॥
Ghar ta terai sabh kichh hai, jis dehi so paavae
घरि त तेरै सभु किछु है जिसु देहि सु पावए ॥
Everything is in Your home. Only the one You give to receives it.
ਸਦਾ ਸਿਫਤਿ ਸਲਾਹ ਤੇਰੀ ਨਾਮੁ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਾਵਏ ॥
Sada sifat salaah teri, naam man vasaavae
सदा सिफति सलाह तेरी नामु मनि वसावए ॥
That person sings Your praises forever and keeps Your Name in the mind.
ਨਾਮੁ ਜਿਨ ਕੈ ਮਨਿ ਵਸਿਆ ਵਾਜੇ ਸਬਦ ਘਨੇਰੇ ॥
Naam jin kai man vasiya, vaaje sabad ghanere
नामु जिन कै मनि वसिआ वाजे सबद घनेरे ॥
In those whose mind the Name lives, the divine music of the Word plays abundantly.
ਕਹੈ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸਚੇ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਕਿਆ ਨਾਹੀ ਘਰਿ ਤੇਰੈ ॥੩॥
Kahai Nanak sache saahib, kya naahi ghar terai ‖3‖
कहै नानकु सचे साहिब किआ नाही घरि तेरै ॥३॥
Says Nanak, O True Master, what is there that is not in Your home?

Pauri 4

Ang 917
About this pauriThe True Name is the Guru's support. It ends every hunger, brings peace, and fulfils every wish. The Guru calls the saints to love the Word.
ਸਾਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਆਧਾਰੋ ॥
Saacha naam mera aadhaaro
साचा नामु मेरा आधारो ॥
The True Name is my support.
ਸਾਚੁ ਨਾਮੁ ਅਧਾਰੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਜਿਨਿ ਭੁਖਾ ਸਭਿ ਗਵਾਈਆ ॥
Saach naam adhaar mera, jin bhukha sabh gavaaiyaan
साचु नामु अधारु मेरा जिनि भुखा सभि गवाईआ ॥
The True Name is my support. It has taken away all my hunger and craving.
ਕਰਿ ਸਾਂਤਿ ਸੁਖ ਮਨਿ ਆਇ ਵਸਿਆ ਜਿਨਿ ਇਛਾ ਸਭਿ ਪੁਜਾਈਆ ॥
Kar saant sukh man aae vasiya, jin ichha sabh pujaaiyaan
करि सांति सुख मनि आइ वसिआ जिनि इछा सभि पुजाईआ ॥
It has come to live in my mind, bringing peace and calm, and has fulfilled all my wishes.
ਸਦਾ ਕੁਰਬਾਣੁ ਕੀਤਾ ਗੁਰੂ ਵਿਟਹੁ ਜਿਸ ਦੀਆ ਏਹਿ ਵਡਿਆਈਆ ॥
Sada kurbaan keeta guru vittahu, jis diyaan ehi vadiaaiyaan
सदा कुरबाणु कीता गुरू विटहु जिस दीआ एहि वडिआईआ ॥
I am forever devoted to the Guru, to whom all this greatness belongs.
ਕਹੈ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਸੁਣਹੁ ਸੰਤਹੁ ਸਬਦਿ ਧਰਹੁ ਪਿਆਰੋ ॥
Kahai Nanak sunahu santahu, sabad dharahu piaaro
कहै नानकु सुणहु संतहु सबदि धरहु पिआरो ॥
Says Nanak, listen, O saints: hold love for the Guru's Word.
ਸਾਚਾ ਨਾਮੁ ਮੇਰਾ ਆਧਾਰੋ ॥੪॥
Saacha naam mera aadhaaro ‖4‖
साचा नामु मेरा आधारो ॥४॥
The True Name is my support.

Pauri 5

Ang 917
About this pauriIn a blessed heart where God has placed His power, divine music plays. The five vices are brought under control, the fear of death is gone, and there is lasting peace.
ਵਾਜੇ ਪੰਚ ਸਬਦ ਤਿਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਸਭਾਗੈ ॥
Vaaje panch sabad, tit ghar sabhaagai
वाजे पंच सबद तितु घरि सभागै ॥
The five kinds of divine music play in that fortunate home (heart).
ਘਰਿ ਸਭਾਗੈ ਸਬਦ ਵਾਜੇ ਕਲਾ ਜਿਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਧਾਰੀਆ ॥
Ghar sabhaagai sabad vaaje, kala jit ghar dhaariya
घरि सभागै सबद वाजे कला जितु घरि धारीआ ॥
The Word plays in that fortunate home, where You, Lord, have placed Your power.
ਪੰਚ ਦੂਤ ਤੁਧੁ ਵਸਿ ਕੀਤੇ ਕਾਲੁ ਕੰਟਕੁ ਮਾਰਿਆ ॥
Panch doot tudh vas keete, kaal kantak maariya
पंच दूत तुधु वसि कीते कालु कंटकु मारिआ ॥
You have brought the five thieves (lust, anger, greed, attachment and ego) under control, and destroyed the torment of death.
ਧੁਰਿ ਕਰਮਿ ਪਾਇਆ ਤੁਧੁ ਜਿਨ ਕਉ ਸਿ ਨਾਮਿ ਹਰਿ ਕੈ ਲਾਗੇ ॥
Dhur karam paaya tudh jin kau, se naam Har kai laage
धुरि करमि पाइआ तुधु जिन कउ सि नामि हरि कै लागे ॥
Those blessed by Your grace from the very beginning become attached to the Lord's Name.
ਕਹੈ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਤਹ ਸੁਖੁ ਹੋਆ ਤਿਤੁ ਘਰਿ ਅਨਹਦ ਵਾਜੇ ॥੫॥
Kahai Nanak tah sukh hoya, tit ghar anhad vaaje ‖5‖
कहै नानकु तह सुखु होआ तितु घरि अनहद वाजे ॥५॥
Says Nanak, there is peace there, and the endless divine melody plays in that home.

Pauri 40

Ang 922
About this pauriThe closing pauri. Listening to this song of bliss fulfils every wish and removes pain and sorrow. Those who hear it and speak it become pure, and the Guru is seen everywhere.
ਅਨਦੁ ਸੁਣਹੁ ਵਡਭਾਗੀਹੋ ਸਗਲ ਮਨੋਰਥ ਪੂਰੇ ॥
Anad sunahu vadbhaageeho, sagal manorath poore
अनदु सुणहु वडभागीहो सगल मनोरथ पूरे ॥
Listen to this song of bliss, O fortunate ones, and all your wishes will be fulfilled.
ਪਾਰਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪਾਇਆ ਉਤਰੇ ਸਗਲ ਵਿਸੂਰੇ ॥
Paarbraham prabh paaya, utre sagal visoore
पारब्रहमु प्रभु पाइआ उतरे सगल विसूरे ॥
I have found the Supreme Lord God, and all my worries are gone.
ਦੂਖ ਰੋਗ ਸੰਤਾਪ ਉਤਰੇ ਸੁਣੀ ਸਚੀ ਬਾਣੀ ॥
Dookh rog santaap utre, suni sachi baani
दूख रोग संताप उतरे सुणी सची बाणी ॥
Pain, illness and suffering have left, by listening to the True Bani.
ਸੰਤ ਸਾਜਨ ਭਏ ਸਰਸੇ ਪੂਰੇ ਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਜਾਣੀ ॥
Sant saajan bhae sarse, poore gur te jaani
संत साजन भए सरसे पूरे गुर ते जाणी ॥
The saints and friends are filled with joy, having come to know it through the Perfect Guru.
ਸੁਣਤੇ ਪੁਨੀਤ ਕਹਤੇ ਪਵਿਤੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਰਹਿਆ ਭਰਪੂਰੇ ॥
Sunte puneet kahte pavit, Satguru rahiya bharpoore
सुणते पुनीत कहते पवितु सतिगुरु रहिआ भरपूरे ॥
Pure are those who listen, and pure are those who recite. The True Guru is present everywhere.
ਬਿਨਵੰਤਿ ਨਾਨਕੁ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਣ ਲਾਗੇ ਵਾਜੇ ਅਨਹਦ ਤੂਰੇ ॥੪੦॥੧॥
Binvant Nanak gur charan laage, vaaje anhad toore ‖40‖1‖
बिनवंति नानकु गुर चरण लागे वाजे अनहद तूरे ॥४०॥१॥
Nanak prays: for those who hold on to the Guru's feet, the endless divine music plays.
Ramkali Mahala 3, Anand. These six pauris are recited in Rehras Sahib and at the close of Sikh gatherings. The English lines show the words as they are recited. Meanings are simple explanations to help you understand each line; for deeper study, Prof. Sahib Singh's Teeka is a trusted source.
